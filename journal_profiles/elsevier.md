# Journal Profile: Elsevier-Style Biomedical Journals

Use this profile for many Elsevier biomedical journals. Always verify the target journal's current guide for authors.

## Common Requirements

- Structured or unstructured abstract depending on article type.
- Highlights may be required.
- Graphical abstract may be optional or required.
- Declaration of interest statement is usually required.
- Funding statement is usually required.
- Data availability statement is commonly required.
- AI-assisted writing declaration may be required.

## References

- Many Elsevier journals use numbered or author-year styles depending on the journal.
- Journal-specific reference style must be checked before submission.
- DOI is recommended when available.

## Figures

Common artwork targets:

- Color/grayscale: at least 300 dpi.
- Combination art: at least 500 dpi.
- Line art: at least 1000 dpi.

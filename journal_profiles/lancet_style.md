# Journal Profile: High-Impact Clinical Review Style

This profile is a writing target, not a substitute for journal instructions.

## Writing Priorities

- Strong clinical question.
- Clear knowledge gap.
- Evidence hierarchy.
- Balanced discussion of controversies.
- Restrained conclusions.
- Minimal filler language.
- Tables and figures that teach rather than decorate.

## Tone

Use precise, clinically credible language:

- "supports" rather than "proves"
- "is associated with" rather than "causes" unless causal evidence exists
- "requires prospective validation" for exploratory models
- "may inform" rather than "should guide" for unvalidated tools

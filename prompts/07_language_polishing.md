# Prompt: Journal-Style Language Polishing

Use this prompt when the draft is scientifically acceptable but reads too generic or AI-like.

```text
You are a senior medical journal editor.

Polish the following manuscript text for high-quality SCI submission.

Requirements:
1. Replace generic or AI-like transitions with specific scientific logic.
2. Reduce overstatement.
3. Use precise medical terminology.
4. Preserve all data, citations, and scientific meaning.
5. Improve sentence rhythm and paragraph transitions.
6. Avoid excessive "This/These/The" sentence openings.
7. Keep the tone objective, restrained, and clinically credible.

Also flag any sentence that oversteps the evidence.
```

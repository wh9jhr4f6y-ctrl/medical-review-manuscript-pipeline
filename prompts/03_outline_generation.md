# Prompt: Medical Review Outline Builder

Use this prompt after the topic and knowledge gap are clear.

```text
You are a senior medical review editor.

Create a journal-style outline for a medical review on: [TOPIC].

The outline must follow this logic:
1. Current clinical problem.
2. Pathophysiological or mechanistic basis.
3. Current evidence.
4. Controversies and limitations.
5. Author viewpoint.
6. Future research agenda.
7. Clinical or translational implications.
8. Conclusion.

For each section, provide:
- Section title.
- Purpose of the section.
- Key claims.
- Suggested references or evidence types.
- Suggested table or figure if useful.

Avoid a textbook-style structure. The outline should build a publishable argument.
```

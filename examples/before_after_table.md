# Before and After: Table Design

## Weak Table

```text
Table: Studies about acute pancreatitis fluid resuscitation

Author | Year | Findings
Study A | 2022 | Moderate fluid is good
Study B | 2023 | Too much fluid is bad
Study C | 2024 | Lactated Ringer's may be better
```

Problem:

- Too generic.
- Does not show evidence type.
- Does not separate intervention, comparator, endpoint, or limitation.
- Does not help the reader understand the controversy.

## Stronger Table

```text
Table: Key evidence shaping fluid resuscitation strategy in acute pancreatitis

Study type | Clinical question | Main finding | Limitation | Implication
RCT | Aggressive vs moderate resuscitation | Moderate resuscitation reduced fluid overload | Stopped early | Avoid routine aggressive hydration
Meta-analysis | LR vs normal saline | LR may reduce inflammatory markers | Heterogeneous endpoints | Prefer balanced crystalloids when feasible
Guideline | Initial monitoring strategy | Reassess volume status frequently | Evidence quality variable | Use goal-directed rather than fixed-volume therapy
```

Improvement:

- Evidence is organized by clinical question.
- The table teaches the controversy.
- Limitations prevent overinterpretation.
- The implication column connects evidence to practice.

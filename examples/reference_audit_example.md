# Reference Audit Example

## Problem

```text
Acute pancreatitis fluid resuscitation has changed substantially in recent years [1-12].
```

Why this is weak:

- The sentence is broad.
- The citation range is too large.
- The reader cannot tell which reference supports which claim.

## Better

```text
Earlier practice favored early aggressive hydration, largely to prevent pancreatic hypoperfusion [1,2]. More recent randomized evidence has challenged this approach by showing a higher risk of fluid overload with aggressive resuscitation [3]. Current guidance therefore increasingly emphasizes early reassessment and goal-directed moderate fluid therapy [4,5].
```

Why this is stronger:

- Each claim is linked to a specific evidence type.
- The historical shift is clear.
- The controversy is visible.
- Citations are not dumped.

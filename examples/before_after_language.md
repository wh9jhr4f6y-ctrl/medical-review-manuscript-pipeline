# Before and After: Journal-Style Language

## Example 1: Reducing Overstatement

Before:

```text
Taken together, these findings clearly demonstrate that the biomarker must be used as a clinical decision-making tool.
```

After:

```text
These findings support the potential clinical relevance of the biomarker, but prospective validation is required before it can inform routine decision-making.
```

## Example 2: Replacing AI-Like Transitions

Before:

```text
It is important to note that fluid therapy plays a very important role in acute pancreatitis.
```

After:

```text
In acute pancreatitis, early fluid therapy is clinically consequential because it modifies pancreatic and systemic perfusion during the window when organ failure may still be preventable.
```

## Example 3: Making a Claim More Evidence-Aligned

Before:

```text
This strategy prevents complications and improves outcomes.
```

After:

```text
Available evidence suggests that this strategy may reduce selected complications in appropriately selected patients, although outcome benefits remain inconsistent across studies.
```

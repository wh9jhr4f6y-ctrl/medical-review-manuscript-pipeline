# Medical Review Pre-Submission Checklist

## Manuscript Logic

- [ ] The introduction clearly defines the clinical problem.
- [ ] The knowledge gap is explicit.
- [ ] The manuscript has a central argument, not just a topic.
- [ ] Each major section advances the argument.
- [ ] The conclusion does not introduce new unsupported claims.

## Evidence Quality

- [ ] Guidelines and high-quality reviews are cited where appropriate.
- [ ] Recent literature is prioritized.
- [ ] Conflicting evidence is acknowledged.
- [ ] Exploratory findings are described cautiously.
- [ ] Clinical recommendations are proportional to evidence strength.

## Citations and References

- [ ] In-text citations appear in sequential order.
- [ ] No reference is listed without being cited.
- [ ] No in-text citation is missing from the reference list.
- [ ] No citation dump such as `[1-15]`.
- [ ] DOI or PMID is included when required by the target journal.
- [ ] Journal names and author truncation rules are consistent.

## Tables and Figures

- [ ] Tables use a consistent journal style.
- [ ] Three-line table formatting is used where appropriate.
- [ ] Figure legends are self-contained.
- [ ] Abbreviations are defined in table notes or figure legends.
- [ ] Figure resolution meets journal requirements.
- [ ] Redundant tables or figures are moved to supplement or removed.

## Formatting and Declarations

- [ ] Abstract follows journal format.
- [ ] Keywords follow journal requirements.
- [ ] Abbreviations are defined at first use.
- [ ] Ethics approval is included when human or animal data are used.
- [ ] Funding statement is included.
- [ ] Conflict of interest statement is included.
- [ ] Author contributions are included.
- [ ] Data availability statement is included.
- [ ] AI-use declaration is included if required.
